Project Overview -
This project is a data analytics case study based on Google Play Store app data.
The objective was to analyze app performance metrics such as ratings, installs, reviews, size, pricing, and categories to uncover insights that can support app development, marketing strategies, and 
product decision-making.

The entire analysis was performed using Tableau, focusing on answering structured business questions across basic, medium, and advanced levels.

Note: This project focuses on analytical problem-solving and insights. No final dashboard was created; instead, all questions were solved using Tableau worksheets and visual analysis.

Tools & Technologies -
Tableau (Desktop/Public) – Data cleaning, analysis, visual exploration  

Dataset Information -
Source:
Google Play Store Apps Dataset
(Kaggle – googleplaystore.csv)



Business Problem -
The company aims to understand:
What makes an app successful on the Google Play Store ,
How user behavior varies across categories and app types ,
The impact of pricing, app size, and updates on ratings and installs

Data Preparation -
The following preprocessing steps were handled inside Tableau:
Converted Installs, Reviews, and Price to numeric formats ,
Handled missing ratings ,
Standardized categories and genres ,
Parsed Last Updated into date format ,
Filtered duplicates where required

Analysis Performed -
 Basic-Level Analysis :
Average rating across all apps ,
Number of unique app categories ,
Distribution of app sizes ,
Free vs Paid app count ,
Most common content rating ,
Top installed apps ,
High-rated apps (Rating ≥ 4.0) ,
Review comparison (Free vs Paid) ,
Category-wise average app size ,
Apps updated in 2018

Medium-Level Analysis -
Correlation between installs and ratings ,
Highest-rated app categories ,
Impact of price on ratings ,
Ratings across content ratings ,
Popular genres with 1M+ installs ,
App update frequency ,
App size vs installs relationship ,
Highly reviewed apps and their ratings ,
Content rating comparison (Free vs Paid) ,
Top categories by total installs

Advanced-Level Analysis -
Top-rated apps vs installs & reviews ,
App update trends over time ,
Binned analysis of installs vs ratings ,
Genre-wise rating trends 

All insights were derived using Tableau visual analysis, filters, calculated fields, and aggregations.

Key Insights -
High installs do not always guarantee high ratings ,
Free apps dominate the Play Store but paid apps often show higher engagement per user ,
Certain categories and genres consistently outperform others ,
App size has a visible impact on installs, especially in low-storage markets ,
Regular updates correlate with better user engagement

Business Impact - 
Helps product teams prioritize high-performing categories ,
Supports marketing teams in targeting popular genres ,
Guides developers on optimal app size and update frequency ,
Informs pricing strategies based on user behavior
